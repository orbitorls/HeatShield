# HeatShield AI — Design Context

## Users
- Primary: Outdoor workers in Thailand (construction, agriculture, school staff)
- Secondary: School administrators, health officials, emergency responders
- Context: Checking heat risk on mobile phones while outdoors in hot conditions
- Job to be done: Quickly assess heat risk and take protective action

## Brand Personality
- **Voice:** Authoritative, Urgent, Warm
- **Tone:** Serious but approachable — this is about health safety, not just data
- **3-word personality:** Protective, Clear, Immediate
- **Emotional goals:** Confidence in the data, urgency to act, warmth in guidance

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Thai Modernist — bold geometric patterns, warm orange palette, asymmetric layouts
- **References:** Jim Thompson House modern Thai aesthetic, Thai temple architecture geometry
- **Anti-references:** Generic dashboard templates, medical/hospital sterile look, overly playful/cartoonish
- **Theme:** Light mode (daytime outdoor use — users are checking while outside in sunlight)

## Design Principles
1. **Clarity over cleverness** — Heat risk information must be instantly readable in bright sunlight
2. **Mobile-first touch targets** — 48px minimum for all interactive elements
3. **Thai cultural resonance** — Warm orange (hue 55) reflects Thai climate and aesthetics
4. **Asymmetric hierarchy** — Important tools (Risk, Events) get visual weight through size and color
5. **Progressive disclosure** — Simple first, reveal sophistication through interaction

## Technical Constraints
- Framework: Next.js with TypeScript
- Styling: Inline styles with CSS variables (no Tailwind classes in components)
- Fonts: Anuphan (display) + Sarabun (body) — Thai-optimized Google Fonts
- Color: OKLCH palette with brand hue 55
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA, prefers-reduced-motion support
